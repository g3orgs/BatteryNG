BatteryNG shows your Android device's live net battery current. Positive values mean current is entering the battery; negative values mean it is discharging. The app includes a flow bar, battery temperature, charging status, and other available battery details. You can switch between milliamperes and amperes. On devices that report the opposite current sign, BatteryNG can detect the polarity automatically or let you correct it manually.

The optional floating overlay shows the current over the launcher and other apps. It requires Android's “Display over other apps” permission. Live measurement and the overlay work without ADB access.

A button in Settings opens Android’s own per-app battery usage report. BatteryNG needs no ADB or usage access for this.

BatteryNG uses network access only for a manually started update check through GitHub. The displayed current is net battery current, not USB input current.
